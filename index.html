Article II — Purpose

Why We
Exist

Blood for Boston aims to increase student blood donation involvement across Greater Boston — through education, on-campus events, community outreach, and partnership with local hospitals.

"We are building more than a club — we are building a culture of care. Mobilizing a new generation of student donors who understand their capacity to impact public health."

Article III — Membership

Open to All
Greater Boston Students

Membership is open to all undergraduate and graduate students — full-time and part-time. No dues required. No barriers. Just a commitment to giving back.

01
Who Can Join

Any undergraduate or graduate student at a Greater Boston-area university. There is no discrimination on the basis of race, religion, gender, political views, sexual orientation, nationality, or disability.

02
How to Join

Attend two general body meetings per semester, or participate in one club-coordinated donation event. No dues are required — optional contributions for outreach materials are welcome.

03
Meetings

General body meetings are held biweekly during the academic semester. Executive Board meetings occur at least once per month. Special meetings may be called with 48 hours' notice.

Article V — Executive Board

Our Leadership
Structure

Six officer roles form the Executive Board. Elections are held at the second-to-last general body meeting of each Spring semester, with winners selected by a majority vote of members present.

President
Chief Executive Officer
Oversees all club operations, facilitates meetings, manages external relations, and leads long-term planning. Must have served on the Executive Board for at least two semesters.
Vice President
Operations & Outreach
Assists the President, manages committee chairs, and coordinates campus-wide outreach initiatives and intercollegiate partnerships.
Secretary
Records & Communications
Keeps meeting minutes, manages member rosters, handles communications, books venues, and oversees election validation alongside the faculty advisor.
Treasurer
Finance & Fundraising
Manages finances, prepares semester budgets, oversees fundraising activities, and presents financial reports each semester in compliance with applicable university policies.
Director of Hospital Coordination
Hospital Liaison
Primary liaison with Boston Children's Hospital and other local donation centers. Leads emergency coordination efforts during regional blood shortages or mass casualty incidents.
Director of Outreach & Events
Campaigns & Community
Coordinates awareness campaigns, blood type screenings, donation drives, and manages social media presence to grow the club's community impact.

Article IX — Emergency Response

Rapid Response
Protocol

In the event of a regional blood shortage, mass casualty incident, or hospital-declared emergency, Blood for Boston activates a coordinated rapid response system.

01

Activate emergency donor notification via SMS and email, alerting members of specific blood types urgently needed.

02

Partner with Boston Children's Hospital and other local hospitals to establish emergency donation centers or extend hours.

03

Prioritize outreach to pre-typed members and facilitate transportation for eligible donors if needed.

04

Collaborate with campus safety offices, university administrations, and local authorities. Maintain an opt-in blood type registry for targeted emergency alerts.

Full Constitution

Our Governing
Articles

Blood for Boston operates under a comprehensive constitution covering membership, governance, elections, finances, expansion, and data privacy.

Article I
Name
The organization is named Blood for Boston, a Greater Boston student initiative spanning multiple universities.
Article II
Aim & Purpose
Increase student blood donation through education, on-campus events, outreach, and hospital liaison.
Article III
Membership
Open to all Greater Boston-area university students. No dues. Attend two meetings or one donation event per semester to be active.
Article IV
Meetings
Biweekly general body meetings. Monthly Executive Board meetings. Special meetings with 48-hour notice.
Article V–VI
Board & Elections
Six-member Executive Board. Spring elections by majority vote. Presidents must have two semesters of board experience.
Article VII
Faculty Advisor
An advisor serves as mentor, assists with hospital communication, and supports event planning.
Article VIII
Amendments
Proposed by any board member or 25% of members. Adopted by two-thirds vote with one week's prior notice.
Article IX
Emergency Response
Rapid response protocol for blood shortages and mass casualty incidents, led by the Hospital Coordination Director.
Article X–XI
Advisory & Committees
Special Advisory Committee of alumni. Standing and ad hoc committees including Public Health Education and Phlebotomy Pathways.
Article XII–XIII
Finances & Dissolution
Transparent financial reporting each semester. Remaining funds upon dissolution go to aligned nonprofits like BCH Blood Donation Center.
Article XIV
Expansion
Framework for partner chapters at other Boston-area universities under the "Blood for Boston" brand.
Article XV & XVII–XVIII
Privacy, Alumni & Founding Board
Member data protected and never shared without consent. Alumni engagement encouraged. Inaugural board serves a two-year extended term.
